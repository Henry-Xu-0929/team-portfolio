# Appendix H: Communication Plan

## 1. Purpose

This Communication Plan defines how information flows between the team, client, supervisor, and other stakeholders. It ensures timely, transparent, and effective communication throughout the project.

---

## 3. Meeting Norms

- **Agenda circulated 24 hours in advance.**
- **Minutes recorded and circulated within 48 hours.**
- **Action Items assigned with owner and deadline.**
- **Outstanding items carried forward to next meeting.**

## 6. Escalation Path

1. **Team-level:** Raise at Daily Stand-up or Sprint Planning.
2. **Scrum Master:** If unresolved within 24 hours.
3. **Team Lead:** If unresolved within 48 hours.
4. **Supervisor:** If unresolved within one week.
